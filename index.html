PERSONAL CORRESPONDENCE09 NOVEMBER

A note for you

Dear Ankiii,

Happy birthday to one of the most interesting contradictions I know. Quietly introverted, unexpectedly chaotic, stubborn in the best way, and somehow capable of turning a casual conversation into a full investigative report.

I still remember our first meet. You sat there, said hello, and looked like the entire concept of meeting people had personally inconvenienced you. Historic moment. Five stars.

It's been genuinely lovely watching you let a softer side show. The sudden baking-and-cooking era is noted. I won't ask who inspired the expansion of the bakery department. I value my life.

I hope you keep your strong, independent self, but also let people care for you. You don't have to be fearless about attachment. Just don't let the fear of getting hurt convince you that closeness isn't worth it. You deserve things that feel safe, kind, and consistent.

May your tea always be hot, the verbal tea always be piping, your playlists remain unapologetically chatpate, and may nobody ever question your right to have very strong opinions.

Also, I remain deeply offended by the persistent "unc" allegations. I demand a formal retraction.

I hope this year brings you good surprises, proper laughter, new memories, and people who make being yourself feel easy.

Happy Birthday, Ankiii.

Stay strong, stay soft where you want to, and keep being your wonderfully chaotic self.

-F
